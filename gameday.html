Iowa Football Game Day Guide

Make the most of a Saturday in Hawkeye country, from pregame plans to the final whistle.

Plan Your Hawkeye Game Day

A football weekend in Iowa City is a chance to experience the energy of Hawkeye country. Whether this is your first visit or you're returning with family and friends, planning your day ahead can make the experience more enjoyable.

Before the Game

Check the official kickoff time, current stadium policies, and parking information before leaving. Arriving early gives you more flexibility to navigate busy streets and meet your group.

Tailgating and Traditions

Set aside time to enjoy the pregame atmosphere with other Hawkeye fans. If you're tailgating, follow the rules of your parking area and keep your plans flexible as crowds grow closer to kickoff.

At Kinnick Stadium

Have your tickets ready, check what items are permitted, and allow time to get to your seats. Be sure to experience the excitement of cheering on the Hawkeyes with the crowd.

Quick Game Day Checklist

Before heading out, confirm your tickets, transportation, weather forecast, and meeting spot. Choose comfortable clothing for the conditions, bring only items allowed in the stadium, and check official University of Iowa Athletics updates for current instructions.

Keep Exploring Iowa City

Make a full weekend of your visit. Find ideas in our Iowa City restaurant guide and explore our things to do in Iowa City page. You can also return to the Hawkeye Visitor Guide homepage.
