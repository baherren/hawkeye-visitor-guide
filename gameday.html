Iowa Football Game Day Guide

From Melrose Avenue to the Hawkeye Wave, here's how to experience a Saturday at Kinnick Stadium.

A Game Day in Hawkeye Country

For visitors coming to an Iowa football game, the experience starts well before kickoff. Wear your black and gold, give yourself time to explore the area around Kinnick Stadium, and plan ahead so you can enjoy the traditions that make a Hawkeye Saturday memorable.

Tailgate on Melrose Avenue

Walk through the area around Melrose Avenue before the game to experience the tailgating atmosphere, meet up with friends, and see Hawkeye fans gathering near the stadium. Follow posted rules in university parking lots and on public streets.

Catch the Hawk Walk

Get near the south end of Kinnick Stadium before kickoff to cheer on the team as players and coaches arrive. The tradition includes passing the Nile Kinnick statue on the way into the stadium. Check the official game day page for that game's timing.

Experience the Hawkeye Wave

At the end of the first quarter, join fans and players in turning toward the nearby University of Iowa Stead Family Children's Hospital to wave to the children and families watching the game. It's one of Iowa football's most meaningful traditions.

See the Marching Band

Make time for the Hawkeye Marching Band's pregame traditions. Iowa Athletics lists a free pregame concert at the Hawkeye Indoor Track, so check the current schedule if you want to catch it.

Plan Your Parking

Parking close to Kinnick fills quickly. The official football parking guide lists game-day options, including free lots with bus shuttles. Check which lots are available for your game, download your mobile parking pass if needed, and expect traffic delays afterward.

Know the Stadium Rules

Kinnick Stadium uses mobile tickets, cashless concessions, and a clear-bag policy. Most backpacks and regular purses are prohibited. Stadium gates generally open 90 minutes before kickoff, so leave time for security screening.

Sample Game Day Plan

  1. Before kickoff: Check the start time, weather, parking options, and stadium policies.
  2. Arrive early: Explore the Melrose Avenue tailgating atmosphere and meet up with your group.
  3. See a tradition: Catch the Hawk Walk or the Hawkeye Marching Band's pregame concert if timing allows.
  4. Head inside: Find your seats in Kinnick and enjoy the pregame excitement.
  5. First quarter: Don't miss the Hawkeye Wave.
  6. After the game: Leave extra time for traffic, or continue your visit downtown.
First-time fan tip: Policies, parking prices, kickoff times, and event schedules can change. Confirm everything on the official Iowa Football Game Day page and official parking guide before attending.
