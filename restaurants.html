Where to Eat in Iowa City

Real local favorites for breakfast, dinner, and everything in between.

Iowa City Restaurant Guide

Visiting Iowa City for a Hawkeye game, campus tour, or weekend with friends? These six restaurants are a great starting point. From a famous pie shake to a downtown burger, there's something for every kind of visit.

Downtown dinner

Pullman Bar & Diner

17 S Dubuque St, Iowa City

For a sit-down meal downtown, Pullman combines American diner favorites with a more modern feel. Burgers, fried chicken, and shareable starters make it a good choice for dinner with family or friends.

Try it for: A relaxed but nicer dinner downtown.

Classic Iowa City stop

Hamburg Inn No. 2

214 N Linn St, Iowa City

This longtime Northside diner is known for breakfast classics, burgers, and its signature pie shakes—pie blended into ice cream. It's a memorable stop if you want a local tradition along with your meal.

Try it for: Breakfast and a famous pie shake.

Breakfast and brunch

Bluebird Diner

330 E Market St, Iowa City

Bluebird is a local breakfast favorite with pancakes, omelets, and comfort-food options. Its casual diner atmosphere works well for families, students, and visitors starting their day in Iowa City.

Try it for: A hearty breakfast before exploring campus.

Burger stop

Short's Burger & Shine

18 S Clinton St, Iowa City

If you're craving a burger downtown, Short's is a familiar Iowa City option. Burgers and fries make it an easy choice for a casual lunch or dinner while visiting the Pedestrian Mall area.

Try it for: A casual burger after walking downtown.

Pizza with a group

Pagliai's Pizza

302 E Bloomington St, Iowa City

Pagliai's is an established Iowa City pizza spot. It's an easy recommendation for groups looking to share a pizza and catch up after a day of visiting campus or cheering on the Hawkeyes.

Try it for: Pizza night with friends or family.

Quick, flavorful lunch

Oasis Falafel

206 N Linn St, Iowa City

For something different from typical game day food, Oasis Falafel is a Northside option known for Mediterranean-style dishes including falafel and hummus. Consider it for a casual lunch while exploring Iowa City.

Try it for: Falafel and a quick lunch.

Where Should You Eat on a Hawkeye Weekend?

Before exploring campus: Start with breakfast at Bluebird Diner or Hamburg Inn No. 2. For a downtown dinner: Consider Pullman or Short's. For a group meal: Try pizza at Pagliai's. For a quick lunch: Oasis Falafel is another option.

Game day tip: Iowa City restaurants can be busy on football weekends. Check current hours and menus directly with each restaurant, and allow extra time before kickoff.
