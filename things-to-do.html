Things to Do in Iowa City

Don't spend your whole Hawkeye weekend sitting in a hotel room. Here's where we'd take first-time visitors.

Six Stops Worth Adding to Your Weekend

From downtown hangouts to fall activities and outdoor exploring, these real Iowa City-area destinations give visitors something specific to plan around. Check each venue's website for current schedules before heading out.

Best for shopping & wandering

1. Explore the Pedestrian Mall

Start in downtown Iowa City's Ped Mall. Browse local shops, grab coffee, people-watch, and explore the walkable streets surrounding College Street. It's an easy way to experience the heart of town before dinner.

Area: Downtown Iowa City

Explore downtown businesses

Best for fall visitors

2. Visit Wilson's Orchard & Farm

Head just north of Iowa City for apple picking, a farm market, fresh bakery items, and seasonal activities. In the fall, it's a fun stop for families or friends who want something besides campus activities.

Address: 4823 Dingleberry Rd NE, Iowa City

See seasonal farm activities

Best for a movie night

3. Catch a Film at FilmScene

Make an evening of it with a movie at Iowa City's nonprofit cinema. FilmScene has two downtown locations and screens a mix of independent films, new releases, and special programming.

Locations: 118 E College St and 404 E College St

Check locations and showtimes

Best for a show

4. See What's On at the Englert Theatre

Looking for nighttime plans beyond dinner? Check the Englert's calendar for concerts, comedy, and other live performances in a historic downtown theater.

Address: 221 E Washington St, Iowa City

Plan a theater visit

Best for exploring campus

5. Walk Around the University of Iowa Campus

Take a walk through campus to get a feel for Hawkeye life. Stroll along the Iowa River, cross a pedestrian bridge, and check out the buildings and outdoor spaces that make the university unique. It is an easy, free activity to fit into a weekend visit.

Area: University of Iowa campus, Iowa City

Explore the University of Iowa

Best for Hawkeye visitors

6. Explore the Pentacrest & Old Capitol

Visit the center of the University of Iowa campus, take photos around the Old Capitol, and learn a little campus history. It's especially convenient if you're already downtown or touring the university.

Address: 21 N Clinton St, Iowa City

Discover more local landmarks

Our Weekend Plan

Friday afternoon: Walk around the Ped Mall and grab dinner downtown. Friday night: Catch a movie at FilmScene or a performance at the Englert. Saturday: Make time for Hawkeye football if there's a home game. Sunday: Visit Wilson's Orchard in season or take a walk around the University of Iowa campus before heading home.
