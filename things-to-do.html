Things to Do in Iowa City

Make the most of your visit with activities and attractions around Hawkeye country.

Explore Iowa City During Your Visit

There are plenty of things to do in Iowa City beyond cheering on the Hawkeyes. Whether you're visiting friends, touring campus, or planning a weekend getaway, our fictional guide can help you organize an enjoyable trip.

Explore Downtown Iowa City

Spend an afternoon exploring downtown Iowa City. Walk through the pedestrian-friendly areas, browse local shops, and enjoy the atmosphere near campus. Downtown is a great place to start when planning a relaxed afternoon.

Visit the University of Iowa Campus

Explore the University of Iowa campus and take in the traditions of Hawkeye country. Visitors can enjoy walking around campus, seeing its buildings and outdoor spaces, and experiencing the college-town environment.

Enjoy a Hawkeye Weekend

Build your weekend around a campus visit, time downtown, and a Hawkeye sporting event. Leave room in your schedule for meals, exploring, and spending time with family or friends.

Plan Your Iowa City Weekend

For a balanced visit, start with a campus walk, head downtown for lunch, and explore shops or local attractions during the afternoon. If you're attending a game, check event details and transportation information ahead of time.
